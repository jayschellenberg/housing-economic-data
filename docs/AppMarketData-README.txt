AppMarketData — the firm's shared market data
=============================================

The folders below feed the firm's web explorers. Use Chrome or Edge.
Nothing is uploaded: the sites read the folders on your own disk.

Housing & Economic Data — ONE pick connects every Local Data tab
----------------------------------------------------------------

Open https://housing-economic-data.vercel.app/#caprates, click
"Connect AppMarketData folder" under the Local Data tabs, and pick:

  - Shared the whole folder with you? Pick this AppMarketData folder.

  - Shared only some of its folders? Dropbox puts each one at the top of
    your Dropbox. Once, make a folder named AppMarketData in your Dropbox,
    drag the shared folders into it (syncing keeps working after the move),
    and pick that. Or connect each one with "Add another folder".

Every tab you have access to loads. Tabs whose folder was not shared with
you say so. On later visits Chrome asks once to re-open the folder; choose
"Allow on every visit" to skip that. Clearing the site's cookies/data makes
the browser forget the folder, so you would pick it once more.

  CapRates\                Cap Rates, Cap Rates vs Interest
  JohnsonReport\           Johnson Report
  RentalData\              Yardi Rental, Rentals.ca, Rent Comparison
  RentalDashboard\         Rental Dashboard
  CommercialAvailability\  Commercial Dashboard
  BrokerageReports\        Brokerage Reports
  FCCFarmland\             FCC Farmland
  Zoning\                  Zoning

Sales sites (button: "Choose export folder...")
-----------------------------------------------

Pick the city/province SUBFOLDER inside SalesData (or this whole
AppMarketData folder; the site steps down to its own subfolder):

  SalesData\Winnipeg\      https://winnipeg-opendata-parcelsearch.vercel.app/
  SalesData\Manitoba\      https://manitoba-opendata-parcelsearch.vercel.app/

Each folder has its own README with details. The folders are refreshed
automatically; do not add, rename or remove files in them.
